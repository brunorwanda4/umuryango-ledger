# UX Psychology & Trust Engineering — Umuryango Ledger

This document details the psychological dynamics of family money management, trust preservation, and reducing emotional friction among family members.

---

## 1. The Psychology of Family Financial Tension

In family finance, conflicts rarely arise from mathematical errors; they arise from **asymmetric visibility**, **ambiguous responsibility**, and **perceived misattribution**:

1. **The Overspending Accusation Trap:**
   - When a custodian withdraws 100,000 RWF from the family MoMo account to hand it to another family member for construction materials, an unrefined ledger records "-100,000 RWF".
   - An elder glancing at this might assume the first custodian spent 100,000 RWF.
   - **Solution:** `umuryango-ledger` labels this as a **Custody Handover (Ihererekanya)** with mutual confirmation, clearly indicating that the money did not vanish but simply transitioned between custody hands.

2. **The Elder Cognitive Burden (Password Fatigue):**
   - Requiring parents or elderly relatives to remember a password, verify a 2FA code, or navigate complex accounting sub-menus leads to immediate abandonment.
   - **Solution:** The **Zero-Login Magic Link** provides read-only transparency with zero barrier to entry. Elders receive a simple link via WhatsApp and tap once to see the entire financial state.

3. **The Shopper's Burden (Frictionless Market Entry):**
   - If logging an expense takes more than 10 seconds or requires 5 mandatory dropdowns, the custodian standing in the market will postpone it and eventually forget.
   - **Solution:** The quick entry dialog requires only Amount + Category + Wallet. Everything else is auto-filled or populated offline.

## 2. The 3 Essential Questions for Elders

Every financial report presented to family elders must directly answer three questions before anything else:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. WHAT CAME IN?   →  Total Inflows & Contributors     │
│ 2. WHAT WENT OUT?  →  Legitimate Family Expenses       │
│ 3. WHO HAS WHAT?   →  Exact Cash / MoMo Custody Balances│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

When an elder sees exactly who holds the cash right now (e.g., *"Mama: 200,000 RWF in hand; Papa: 150,000 RWF in hand; MoMo: 1,450,000 RWF"*), peace of mind is restored and trust is maintained.

---

## 3. Dignity in Debt & Project Contributions

1. **Transparent Projects (Positive Reinforcement):**
   - Progress bars visually celebrate collective achievements (e.g., *"Parents' House Renovation: 24% completed"*).
   - Shows all family members pulling together toward a common goal.
2. **Dignified Debt Reminders:**
   - Debts and family loan commitments are tracked objectively with due dates, avoiding aggressive or accusatory language.
   - Localized status badges remind members of obligations respectfully.
